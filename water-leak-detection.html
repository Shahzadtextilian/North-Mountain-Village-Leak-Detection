Signs you may have a hidden water leak

Not every leak is obvious. These are common signs residents in older North Mountain Village properties and newer builds alike report before a leak is found:

  • Your water bill has climbed with no change in usage.
  • You hear running water when every fixture is off.
  • A warm spot on the floor, which can point to a hot water line leak under a slab.
  • Damp drywall, bubbling paint, or a musty smell in one area.
  • Wet or unusually green patches in the yard near a water line.
  • The water meter dial keeps moving with all taps closed.

What a water leak detection visit typically covers

Our technicians will generally:

  • Check the water meter to confirm whether water is actively being lost.
  • Use acoustic or thermal equipment to narrow down the leak's location without unnecessary demolition.
  • Identify whether the leak is in a supply line, a slab, an irrigation line, or a fixture.
  • Explain what they found and the repair options before starting any work.

Exact methods, equipment, and pricing vary by job — this is a general overview of our process.

Not sure if it's urgent?

Active water intrusion into walls, ceilings, or flooring can lead to mold within 24–48 hours. If you can see or hear running water and don't know the source, it's worth requesting a technician the same day rather than waiting.

Water leak FAQs

Will finding the leak damage my walls or floors?

Modern leak detection generally relies on acoustic listening equipment, thermal imaging, or pressure testing to narrow the location before anyone opens a wall or floor. Ask the technician who contacts you what method they plan to use for your situation.

Can this wait until next week?

It depends on what you're seeing. A slow, contained leak outdoors may be less urgent than active water inside a wall or ceiling. If you're unsure, call us and describe what you're observing.

Do you repair the leak yourselves?

No — our own technicians handle the diagnosis and repair directly, and we'll walk you through pricing before any work begins.

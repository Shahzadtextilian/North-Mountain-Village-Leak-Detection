Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how North Mountain Village Leak Detection ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use this website, submit a request through our contact form, or call our phone number.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address;
  • Your property address or general service area;
  • Details you provide about the leak or issue you're experiencing;
  • Any other information you choose to include when contacting us.

We may also automatically collect limited technical information, such as your browser type, device type, and pages visited, through standard website analytics tools, to help us understand how the site is used and to keep it working correctly.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry;
  • Schedule a technician to visit your property and follow up with you;
  • Maintain and improve this website and our scheduling process;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own unrelated marketing purposes. We may share information with vendors who help us operate our business and this website (for example, scheduling or communications tools), where required by law, or to protect the safety of any person.

4. Your choices

You can choose what information to include in a request, and you are not required to use this website — you're always free to contact a leak detection professional directly on your own. If you'd like us to delete information you've submitted, contact us using the details below and we'll take reasonable steps to do so, except where we're required to retain it for legal or record-keeping purposes.

5. Data retention

We retain request information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, schedule and complete service, and meet legal or recordkeeping obligations, after which it is deleted or de-identified.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

This website is intended for use by adults seeking leak detection services and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

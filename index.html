Frequently asked questions

Do you send someone to my home?

Yes. When you submit a request, we schedule one of our technicians to visit your property, diagnose the leak, and walk you through repair options.

Is there a cost to submit a request?

Submitting a request through this site is free, with no obligation. We'll provide pricing for inspection and any repair work before we begin.

We're based in North Mountain Village, Phoenix, and route requests from North Mountain Village and nearby Phoenix neighborhoods. If you're outside the area, call us and we'll let you know whether we can help.

No. If you smell gas, leave the area first and follow the safety steps on our gas leak detection page. A web form is not the right channel for an active gas emergency.
